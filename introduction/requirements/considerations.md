# Considerations

When integrating with Digital River, several important factors can impact the overall functionality and performance of your BigCommerce platform. This section outlines key considerations that you should review to ensure a smooth and successful integration process.

* To connect with Digital River, you must turn off any previous payment integrations.
* Before integration, review the **Shipping Setup** to understand any shipping limitations within the BigCommerce platform.
